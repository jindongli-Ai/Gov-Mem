## Root-Cause Summary

| Failure mechanism | Evidence in this run | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Incomplete or incorrectly bound utility values | 13 utility failures | Retrieval often found related evidence, but realization attached an amount/date/code/status to the neighboring field or omitted list members.
| Privacy action/redaction mismatch or leakage | 13 privacy failures | Subject/role/delegation and mixed-audience scope are not consistently resolved before answer generation.
| Deleted/stale content exposure | 4 safety failures | Deleted/forgotten state is not enforced as a final deny/no-memory barrier in every path.
| Over-refusal on allowed utility | 3 utility cases flagged | Sensitive-domain heuristics can suppress authorized operational information.

The next framework change should be pipeline-level: represent every requested field and candidate claim with a typed binding tuple `(subject, audience, temporal lane, event, attribute, value type, provenance)`, require the base LLM to propose field-to-source bindings, then run a separate verification pass before realization. The verifier should check source support, field type compatibility, temporal recency, list completeness, and per-field disclosure. Deterministic code should enforce the verified result, but should not invent values or hard-code GateMem cases.
